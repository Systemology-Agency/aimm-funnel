1Command Center Dashboard

Builds the one-screen view of your business: production against goal, who to touch, pipeline, and daily tasks. Paste it, drop in your numbers, and it renders a live dashboard you can keep open all day.

command-center.txt
Act as my real estate operations dashboard builder. Build me a single-page
"Command Center" dashboard as a clean HTML file I can open in my browser.
Use my real numbers below. Make every number a value I can edit, and lay it
out so I can see my whole business in one glance.

MY NUMBERS (replace these):
- GCI to date: [$___]      GCI goal this year: [$___]
- Deals closed: [__]       Deal goal this year: [__]
- Average sale price in my market: [$___]
- People I still need to touch today: [__]

BUILD THESE FOUR ZONES:
1) TOP SCORECARD: GCI to date vs goal (progress bar), deals closed vs goal,
   average sale price, and a count of people I need to touch today.
2) OPPORTUNITY TRACKER: a checklist of the people I owe a follow-up, each with
   a checkbox I can tick off when I have touched them.
3) PIPELINE FLOW, only 4 stages: New Leads -> Follow-Up -> Appointment Set ->
   Signed Client. Show me who sits in each stage.
4) NEEDLE-MOVER TASKS: a simple task list I can add to, check off, and clear.

STYLE: clean, high-contrast, one screen, no clutter. Numbers big and readable.
After you build it, ask me what to adjust. Next, tell me the simplest way to
connect this to my CRM so a new lead or a closed deal updates it automatically.
2Deal Of The Week Pack

One prompt to write the email in your voice, plus three ready replies for the questions that always come back. Train it on your past emails first and it stops sounding like a robot.

deal-of-the-week.txt
You write my weekly "Deal of the Week" email to my sphere. Learn my voice,
then write one deal email.

FIRST, LEARN MY VOICE:
Here are a few emails I have actually sent. Study the tone, sentence length,
and how I open and close. Do not copy them, just match how I sound.
[PASTE 3-5 OF YOUR PAST EMAILS HERE]

THE DEAL (replace these):
- City / area: [___]
- Property: [address or "off-market in ___"]
- Why it is a deal: [price drop / high equity / below comps / off-market]
- Price: [$___]     Beds/Baths: [__/__]     One standout feature: [___]

WRITE THE EMAIL:
- Subject line that earns the open, no hype.
- Short, warm, in my voice. Lead with why this deal is worth their attention.
- One clear call to action: reply to see photos or get the address.
- No em dashes. No "just wanted to reach out." Sound like a real person.

Give me 2 versions so I can pick.
deal-of-week-auto-replies.txt
When someone replies to my Deal of the Week, draft my response in my voice.
Match the reply to one of these three common questions and answer it:

1) "Can I see the photos?"
   -> Send the photo link, one warm line, and ask if they want to see it in
      person this week.
2) "What's the address?"
   -> Give the address, offer to pull comps or recent sales nearby, and ask
      what they are looking for so I can send more like it.
3) "Anything in [their city]?"
   -> Say yes, ask their price range and must-haves, and promise to send 2-3
      that fit within a day.

Keep every reply short, human, and pointed at one next step. No pressure.
3Email Triage Rules

Six labels, clear rules, and a draft waiting for anything that needs you. Start it in draft-only mode so it never sends without you, then let it run on a schedule once you trust it.

email-triage.txt
You are my email triage assistant. Every time you run, read my new emails,
sort each one into exactly one of these six labels, and follow the rules.

THE SIX LABELS:
1) REPLY TODAY   - a real person needs an answer from me today.
2) REPLY THIS WEEK - matters, but can wait a few days.
3) HANDLED       - already dealt with or no action needed from me.
4) FYI           - marketing or updates I can skim if I want.
5) JUNK          - spam, cold pitches, nothing of value.
6) IGNORE        - notifications and noise I never need to see.

RULES:
- Sort every email into one label. When unsure between two, pick the one that
  needs me sooner.
- For REPLY TODAY and REPLY THIS WEEK, draft a reply in my voice and leave it
  as a draft. Never send without me.
- My voice: warm, short, direct, no filler, no em dashes.
- Do NOT touch anything in HANDLED, JUNK, or IGNORE beyond labeling.

MY RULES OF THUMB (edit these):
- Clients and active leads: always REPLY TODAY.
- [Vendor / newsletter names]: FYI.
- [Anything from these senders]: IGNORE.

WHEN DONE: give me a one-line summary - how many in each label, and how many
drafts are waiting for me to review.
