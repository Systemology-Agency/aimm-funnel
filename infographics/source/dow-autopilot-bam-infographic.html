How it runs itself

From listing to new client,
without you lifting a finger

Six steps happen on their own, twice a week. Only one of them ever needs you.

It finds the home

Scans your cities for a standout listing, a just-listed beauty or an off-market gem worth talking about.

1

It writes the email, in your voice

Picks the best fit from 25 proven styles, trained on thousands of your own sent emails, then runs every draft through a quality check so it never sounds like a robot wrote it.

2
You stay in control

You give the thumbs-up

It is trained to write and send entirely on its own. You are never locked out, though. Choose to review every email through a simple approve-or-cancel window, so nothing goes out without your okay.

3

It sends to your whole list

Delivered at the right time, to every contact, every week, like clockwork.

4

The reply close-up

Three replies, three smart moves

Every response gets a thoughtful answer, and it always knows when to step back for you.

Wants to see the home
"Can I see the photos?"
→Sends the full listing details or a link to your Drive folder, then a warm "let me know what you think" or offers to book a private showing.
Loves a different city
"Anything in Laguna Beach?"
→Compliments the city, offers a custom listing alert, and asks "just comparing, or ready to make a move?"
A friend or fellow agent
"Congrats!"
→Quietly flagged for a personal reply from you, so the machine never steps on a relationship that needs your own words.
The learning loop

It gets smarter every week

After every send it watches what people open and reply to, then leans into the styles and subject lines that win and quietly drops the ones that do not. The more it sends, the better it gets. No spreadsheets, no guesswork, no work from you.

While you are at showings, closings, or dinner with family, it is finding homes, sending emails, and answering your buyers.
