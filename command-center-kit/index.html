System Requirements

What you need before you build. Three required pieces. One optional piece that makes it live.

You are not buying software. You are assembling three things you can get today, then pointing an AI at them.

Required

1. A Claude subscription

A paid Claude plan (Pro or Max). This is the intelligence that builds and runs everything. Max is worth it if you will run this daily.

The brain
Required

2. The Claude desktop app

Installed on your Mac or PC. It is where you talk to your Command Center, paste the prompt, and open the dashboard.

The front door
Required

3. Cowork

Claude's agentic workspace. This is what lets Claude actually do the work on a schedule and keep files, not just chat. Your Command Center lives and refreshes here. It can run as a live artifact here too, so it's always up to date without you touching it.

The workspace
Optional

4. A local AI CRM ("ACRM")

A lightweight CRM that lives with your workspace and holds your leads and deals. Connect it and your dashboard stops being a picture and becomes live: add a lead or close a deal, and every number updates on its own. Its pipeline stages must match the Command Center's, one for one. You can also use any third-party CRM with API access instead: pull contacts and notes, and connect it two ways so it updates your CRM and your Command Center seamlessly.

The live data source

Without the ACRM you still get a working Command Center you update by talking to it. With it, the whole thing runs itself.

04

The Pipeline (Keep It Identical Everywhere)

Four stages plus a nurture bucket. This is the one rule that matters: your Command Center and your CRM must use the exact same stages, or your numbers will lie.
Stage 1
New Leads
Fresh, unworked. The hot prospects who just came in.
Stage 2
Follow-Up
You are chasing an appointment. The work that makes the money.
Stage 3
Appointment
Meeting set. Prep them, show up, don't drop the ball.
Stage 4
Signed Client
Under contract or represented. Keep touching them to close.
+ Nurture: anyone who goes cold or isn't ready drops here instead of disappearing. They keep getting touched until they re-enter Stage 1.

You can rename these to match how you work. What you cannot do is have one set of stages in your dashboard and a different set in your CRM. Same names, same colors, same order, everywhere.

The one rule: your CRM (or lead list) is the single source of truth. The Command Center is a live view of it, not a second copy. Change the source, the view reflects it going forward, automatically. A daily refresh rebuilds the screen so you never touch it by hand.

Wiring in your own data sources is the real work here. The best data sources are local to your AI: for the long term, that means building an internal AI CRM and an internal transaction manager. These are exactly what we build together inside the 6-week AIMM program.

The Do-It-Yourself Prompt

Paste this into the Claude desktop app or Cowork. It interviews you first, then builds your Command Center wired to your answers. No code required.

It will ask you these before it builds anything:

  1. Your name and business name
  2. Your market (cities / farm areas)
  3. GCI to date and your GCI goal for the year
  4. Deals closed and your deal goal
  5. Your average sale price
  6. Your pipeline stage names (or use the standard 4 + Nurture)
  7. The 3–5 numbers you most want to see first
  8. Whether you have a CRM / lead list to connect
  9. Your brand colors (or "pick a clean default")
  10. Anything else you check or do every single day

You're Done When… + Glossary

How to know it's working, the words you'll hear, and the one safety rule.

Acceptance checklist. Your Command Center is real when:

  • You open one screen and see your top numbers, today's actions, your pipeline, and your tasks.
  • Your pipeline stages are identical in the dashboard and your CRM.
  • Closing a deal or adding a lead updates the numbers without you editing the screen.
  • It rebuilds itself on a schedule (a daily refresh), so you never update it by hand.
  • You can run your day from it alone: who to touch, what to do, where every deal stands.
One safety rule, no exceptionsNever paste passwords into a chat. Connect tools through their official sign-in or a password manager. Keep anything that sends or changes data in draft mode until you trust it.

Plain-English glossary

Agent: an AI you train once that then does work for you, like an employee.
Skill: a saved recipe the agent runs the same way every time.
CRM / ACRM: where your leads and deals live. ACRM = one that runs locally with your workspace.
Cowork: Claude's workspace where your agent keeps files and runs on a schedule.
Pipeline: the stages a lead moves through, from new to closed.
Source of truth: the one place data really lives. Everything else is a view of it.
