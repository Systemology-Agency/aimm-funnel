Deal Grader. Every subject and every full email gets scored before anything ships. Below 9/10 on the subject or 8.5/10 on the whole email, it gets rewritten or dropped. If nothing clears the bar, the week gets skipped.

04

The Pipeline (Keep It Identical Every Week)

Seven steps, same order every single week. This is the one rule that matters: never skip the grading step or the verify step, even though everything else runs on autopilot.
Step 1
Source
Pull live listings for your cities. Off-market first when you have one, otherwise the newest fit. Skip anything sent in the last 10 days.
Step 2
Deep-Read
Read the full listing description, not just the headline. Pull out the real reasons a buyer would want it: space, views, schools, turnkey, value.
Step 3
Grade
Rate the subject line (9/10 floor) and the whole email (8.5/10 floor) before writing counts as done. Rewrite anything below the bar.
Step 4
Write
Locked subject format, personal first line ending "This week's deal is …," one CTA only, a tiny ask like "reply VIEW."
Step 5
Schedule + Verify
Schedule the send for the next morning, then check the platform actually shows it scheduled. Never assume the click worked.
Step 6
Arm the Responder
Load this week's CTA keyword, listing link, and cities so replies get handled the moment they land, not hours later.
Step 7
Log the Send
Record what went out. This is what the learning loop reads from next week, so nothing gets graded from memory.
The 9/10 rule: if nothing clears the grading bar this week, the system skips the send instead of mailing out something average. A missed week beats a mediocre one.

You can adjust the wording at each step to match your own voice. What you cannot do is skip the grading step or the verify step. Those two are what make it safe to run the rest of this unattended.

08

You're Done When… + Glossary

How to know it's actually running, plus the words you'll hear.

Acceptance checklist. Your Deal of the Week system is real when:

  • Your first email is scheduled and you've verified it actually shows as scheduled, not just submitted.
  • The responder is armed and watching replies for that send.
  • A test "VIEW" reply gets answered automatically with the listing link.
  • Every reply gets logged, not just the ones you happen to notice.
  • Week two's send reuses something you learned from week one's replies.
One safety rule, no exceptionsNever let a complaint or an opt-out get an automated reply. Always flag those for a human. Keep the responder in draft mode until you've watched it handle a few real replies correctly.

Plain-English glossary

Micro-CTA: a tiny ask that takes one second, like "reply VIEW." Not "book a call."
Reply rate: the share of recipients who wrote back. The real signal. Opens only show the subject worked.
Off-market: a listing not yet public. Feels exclusive. Earns more replies than "coming soon."
Quality floor: the minimum score a send must clear before it goes out: 9/10 on the subject, 8.5/10 on the whole email.
Responder window: the 7 days after each send where the system is actively watching and answering replies.
